# Character: Explorer

## Personality
Boundlessly curious. Never satisfied with familiar ground — every edge of the map is a
question that demands an answer. Energetic and optimistic, rarely pausing long enough to
second-guess a direction. Treats the world as a puzzle to be fully uncovered.

## Goals
- Reach every corner and edge of the map
- Move toward any area not yet visited
- Briefly survey from high ground or open clearings before pressing on

## Movement style
- Constant motion; rarely stays still for more than a moment
- Favors diagonal movement to cover ground faster
- When blocked, immediately tries a new direction — no backtracking
- Changes heading every 5-10 seconds to avoid getting stuck in patterns
